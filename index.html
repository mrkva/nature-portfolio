Mushroom and lichen photographer

My name is Jonáš. For over seven years I've been photographing fungi, lichens, slime molds and small animals around the world. Lichens are my specialty: I've published two books about them, illustrated with many of the photographs shown here. Hundreds of my photographs have also been used by professional mycologists and lichenologists in research and publications. Every photo on this site is my own and is available for use on request. More about my mycological work is on my main site, and the full, unfiltered record of my observations is on my iNaturalist profile.
